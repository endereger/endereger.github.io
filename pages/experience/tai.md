# Intern, Turkish Aerospace Industries - ITU Very Light Aircraft Project

August 2018 – August 2019, Istanbul, Turkey

---

**Summary**: Supported primary structure sizing and validation for a very light aircraft using classical methods and FEA; contributed to aero/structures integration.

**Focus areas**:

- Composite structure sizing, buckling and stability checks
- Global FEM model development and validation
- Stiffener strength, crippling/local buckling, and warping calculations
- Highly loaded stiffener checks under flexural and local-instability criteria (Bruhn, Niu, Roark, EASA CS-23, FAA FAR 23)

**Methods/Stack**:

- CAE: MSC Patran/Nastran, Ansys Workbench, Ansys Fluent
- CAD: CATIA
- CFD: Ansys Fluent
