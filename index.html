About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Virginia Tech. My work is at the intersection of computational materials science, physics-informed machine learning, numerical simulation, and optimization. I develop physics-guided and data-driven methods for modeling complex material behavior, designing microstructures, and solving inverse problems in multiphysics systems.

Email Me View Publications Research Interests
Research Interests
  • Physics-informed machine learning for materials modeling, including microstructure-sensitive deformation modeling and inverse design.
  • Computational materials science and multiphysics simulation using finite element methods, molecular dynamics, and high-performance computing workflows.
  • Surrogate modeling and optimization for high-dimensional scientific problems, including neural-network-based predictors and evolutionary optimization methods.
  • Magnetic materials and micromagnetic/microstructure design, including optimization of magnetic lattice systems and uncertainty-aware modeling.
  • Acoustic holography and transcranial focused ultrasound source design using forward simulation, adjoint-style updates, and metaheuristic optimization.
